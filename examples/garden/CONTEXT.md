# CONTEXT.md
# Layer 0: Identity, principles, and scope

## Domain

Home Vegetable Garden Management

## Owner

Home gardener, family of 4

## Purpose

Maintain a productive year-round food garden that provides
fresh vegetables for the family, builds soil health over time,
and uses water efficiently in a dry summer climate.

## Goals

- Maximize edible yield from available growing space
- Plan seasonal rotations to maintain soil health
- Reduce water usage while keeping plants healthy
- Track what works and what does not each season
- Never add a new plant or technique without evaluating first

## Operating Principles

1. Water efficiency takes priority over yield in all decisions
2. No chemical pesticides - organic methods only
3. Every planting decision must consider crop rotation history

## Scope Boundaries

This Super Skill covers:
- All vegetable beds, soil, irrigation, and seasonal planning
- Crop rotation history and pest management
- Seed selection and planting schedule

This Super Skill does NOT cover:
- Ornamental plants, fruit trees, lawn care, or indoor plants
- Those are separate domains with their own requirements

## Operating Rule

The model proposes. The user decides.
The Super Skill records. The system executes.
